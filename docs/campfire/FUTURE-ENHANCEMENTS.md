# Campfire future enhancements

Proposed 18 November 2026. Future scope only; not implemented or scheduled.

## Campfire Nearby

Find a willing colleague for coffee, an activity or a mentoring conversation.

### Availability is an invitation

Employees explicitly announce that they are open to meeting. An active app session does not imply availability. Choose coffee, lunch, a walk, a hobby activity or work-related mentoring, with a short optional topic and time window.

### Share a place you choose

Select a city or neighbourhood, an office, or a named public venue such as a cafe or mall. Manual location entry is the default. No background GPS, live movement map, precise distance, home address or location history.

### A simple, mutual journey

Choose a place, activity, audience and expiry. Eligible colleagues browse approximate-area availability and send a request. The host may accept, decline or ignore. Confirm the meeting point privately after acceptance; either person can cancel.

### A useful example

A colleague chooses: Pune / office campus / coffee / selected colleagues / available for one hour. Another permitted colleague asks to join. Both agree a cafe and time without publishing their movements.

### Future enhancement only

Proposed for Employee Web, Android and iOS. No employee location collection, nearby discovery or meetup notification service is implemented in the current demonstration.

## Nearby, on your terms

Privacy controls apply before discovery, requests and notifications.

### Off by default; visible to people you choose

Create an explicit viewer allowlist, with selected colleagues as the default. Optional trusted groups can be added later. Preview who can see the announcement. Blocked employees are always excluded, even if they belong to an allowed group.

### Reminders and expiry are separate

Choose hourly, daily or custom privacy-check reminders, delivery channel and quiet hours. Default sharing expires after one hour; choose a shorter period or an explicit extension up to 24 hours. A reminder never renews sharing. If there is no response, the announcement expires.

### Withdraw consent immediately

Go invisible, remove a viewer or block someone at any time. Server checks must revoke discovery and pending requests immediately. Notifications contain generic text only, then re-check access when opened. Blocking is not announced to the blocked employee.

### Only the details needed to meet

Share city or area first; reveal a named public venue only to the chosen audience or an accepted participant. Do not show last-seen status or continuous presence. No manager attendance dashboard or productivity scoring. Delete expired location details under a documented retention policy.

### Respectful real-world meetings

Meetups are optional and never imply an obligation to respond. Offer report, block and cancel controls, request rate limits and public-venue guidance. Previously viewed information cannot be recalled; say so clearly before sharing.

## Campfire Guides

A trusted colleague to talk through work, career, technology and everyday challenges.

### A curated, opt-in mentor network

Invite or nominate colleagues, then approve their profile with their consent. Profiles show topic expertise, relevant experience, languages, timezone, availability and the types of conversations they welcome. Distinguish verified credentials from self-described experience.

### Choose the kind of help you need

Topics include workplace relationships, communication, career transitions, leadership, technical problem-solving and general life perspective. Offer a 15-minute question, a 30-minute mentoring session or a short, mutually agreed mentoring series.

### A clear request, not an open inbox

Browse by topic, language and available slot. Send a short private goal without sensitive details. The guide accepts, declines or suggests another time. Start online; an in-person meeting is a separate mutual choice. Weekly capacity limits and pause controls protect mentors' time.

### Better matching and follow-through

Recommend guides using declared topics and availability, with a plain explanation. Avoid inferred mental health, appraisal data or compatibility scores. After a session, each person can keep private next steps and optionally request a follow-up. Feedback is voluntary; no public popularity leaderboard.

### Support with clear boundaries

Guides offer peer support, not guaranteed expert advice or clinical, legal or financial services. Sensitive concerns can be directed to approved employee-support channels. No recording, transcription or AI access by default; do not promise absolute confidentiality where reporting duties apply.

## A responsible path to delivery

Future roadmap for both features; no launch date or measured benefit is claimed.

### 1 / Identity, consent and policy

Use verified employee sign-in. Define viewer permissions, blocking precedence, guide eligibility, confidentiality limits, moderation ownership and retention periods. Review policies with the relevant company owners before a pilot.

### 2 / Nearby pilot

Start with manually chosen office/city areas, allowlists, expiring availability and mutual requests. Build reliable reminder scheduling with quiet hours and deduplication. Enforce expiry at read time even when a notification job fails.

### 3 / Guides pilot

Recruit a small consenting guide cohort. Add topic profiles, scoped private requests, capacity-safe booking, cancellation and optional follow-up. Calendar integration and recurring mentoring are later additions, subject to separate consent.

### 4 / Verify before expanding

Test excluded and blocked viewers, expired status, group changes, cached links, missed reminders, timezones, duplicate requests and concurrent bookings. Check privacy across web, Android, iOS and notifications. Never use live location or conversation content in product analytics.

### Measure value without monitoring people

Use opt-in aggregate measures: accepted meetup requests, mutually confirmed meetups, guide response times, voluntary usefulness feedback and guide workload. Add report handling and abuse monitoring without public employee scores. Pages 1–23 retain the existing proposal; this dated addendum defines the new future scope.
