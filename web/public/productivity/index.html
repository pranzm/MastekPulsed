PAUSE & RESET

Find a little space

Box breathing: breathe in, hold, breathe out, hold — four seconds each. Stay comfortable; stop and breathe naturally if it feels uncomfortable.

Mindfulness: notice your breath or the sounds around you. Gently return your attention when it wanders. You can stop at any time.
